About

I'm a Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering MS student at NYU, working as a Research Assistant at the Generalizable Robotics & AI Lab (CILVR). My focus is building navigation and perception systems that actually work in the real world — not just in simulation.

My main project is the YOR mobile manipulator: an open-source, sub-$10K platform for mobile manipulation research. I built its entire navigation stack — from VINS-Mono SLAM and voxel mapping to path planning and pure-pursuit control — validated to 12mm odometry accuracy in live indoor environments.

arXiv:2602.11150 · YOR: Your Own Mobile Manipulator
Contributing author · Feb 2026

Before NYU, I was a Graduate Apprentice at Bosch Chassis Systems, where I won 1st place in Bosch's MDP Global Championship — an internal innovation competition across international teams — for a sensor-fusion analytics pipeline.

I'm actively looking for full-time roles in robotics software engineering, autonomy, or robot learning. Available May 2026.
