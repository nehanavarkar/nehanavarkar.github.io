About

I'm a Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering MS student at NYU, working as a Research Assistant at the Generalizable Robotics & AI Lab (CILVR). My work sits at the intersection of autonomous navigation, visual SLAM, and embedded perception — building systems that work in the real world.

Previously I was a Graduate Apprentice at Bosch Chassis Systems, where I led 23 digitization projects and won 1st place in Bosch's MDP Global Championship. I'm excited about making capable robots accessible and affordable.

I'm actively looking for full-time roles in robotics software engineering at mission-driven startups and mid-size companies.
